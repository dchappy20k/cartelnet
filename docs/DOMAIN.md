# Domain Model & Entity Architecture

## 1. Domain Entities
CartelNet models public procurement risk across these core entities:

### Organization (`Organization`)
- Multi-tenant boundary.
- Types: `GOVERNMENT_AUTHORITY` vs `COMPANY`.
- Owns all tenders, bids, signals, investigations, and audit logs.

### User & Membership (`User`, `OrganizationMembership`)
- RBAC roles: `ADMIN`, `PROCUREMENT_OFFICER`, `COMPLIANCE_ANALYST`, `INVESTIGATOR`, `VIEWER`.
- Scoped strictly to organization tenant.

### Tender (`Tender`)
- Procurement tender with ID, authority, title, estimated value, publication date, closing date, status (`OPEN`, `AWARDED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `CLOSED`).
- Aggregate computed risk score (0–100) and severity band (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`).

### Company / Bidder (`Company`)
- Corporate entity with legal name, normalized name, tax/reg number, jurisdiction, registered address, incorporation date.

### Director (`Director`)
- Individual associated with corporate governance (`CompanyDirector` association with start_date, end_date, role).

### Address (`Address`)
- Physical registered address with normalized hash for clustering.

### Bid (`Bid`)
- Bid submission linking `Company` to `Tender` with bid amount, submission timestamp, status (`AWARDED`, `RUNNER_UP`, `REJECTED`, `WITHDRAWN`), and cover-bid indicators.

### Risk Signal (`RiskSignal`)
- Specific anomalous finding generated by a risk detector.
- Attributes: detector code, severity, confidence (0–100), score contribution, explanation, evidence references.

### Evidence (`Evidence`)
- Specific data record or artifact proving the signal (source table, source row ID, snapshot data, description).

### Investigation Case (`Investigation`)
- Formal review case linked to a Tender or Company Cluster.
- Status: `NEW`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `WAITING`, `ESCALATED`, `CLOSED`.
- Priority: `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`.
- Manages investigator assignments, notes, evidence attachments, and report exports.
